Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve for residential electrical?

We provide residential services within 100 miles of Vienna — service calls, panel upgrades, and remodel wiring. Just outside that range? Call us anyway.

Do you travel for commercial or industrial projects?

Yes. Creative Wiring Solutions is available nationwide for commercial, industrial, and marine electrical projects of any scale.

Do you offer financing for electrical work?

We do. We work with trusted financing partners to make quality electrical work accessible. Mention financing when you request your quote — no pressure, no obligation.

What generator brands and types do you install?

We install whole-home standby generators and commercial systems, and provide ongoing maintenance. We'll help you find the right size and system for your property.

Can you handle LED retrofits in large commercial spaces?

Absolutely. We handle LED projects for offices, warehouses, retail spaces, and industrial facilities. We manage everything from wiring to the final fixture install.
