Questions

What areas do you serve for residential electrical?

We provide residential services — service calls, panel upgrades, remodel wiring — within 100 miles of Vienna. Need something just outside that range? Call us anyway; we'll do our best.

Do you travel for commercial or industrial projects?

Yes. Creative Wiring Solutions is available nationwide for commercial, industrial, and marine electrical projects. No job is too far for the right project.

Do you offer financing for electrical work?

We do. We work with trusted financing partners to make quality electrical work accessible. Mention financing when you request your quote — there's no obligation and no pressure.

What generator brands and types do you install?

We install whole-home standby generators and commercial systems, and we provide ongoing maintenance and service. We'll help you choose the right size and system for your property and budget.

Absolutely. We handle LED retrofit projects for offices, warehouses, retail spaces, and industrial facilities. LED upgrades can dramatically cut energy costs, and we manage everything from wiring to final fixture install.
